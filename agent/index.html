The Uderia Agent
an entity, not a chatbot

An agent in Uderia is a configuration, not a program: a constitution, senses, memory, and a brain to reason with — everything it is, bounded by a single membrane of trust.

Its life, in five acts
Act IBorn

Born, not assembled.

A Uderia agent is born with a genome: a reasoning engine, a model, its data sources and its class.

In the trust story: Act I — Born, then trusted · badge · compose · validate · sign · travel.

Every agent inherits a DNA at birth: the Fusion Optimizer — a reasoning engine that plans strategically, executes tactically, corrects itself, and learns from every success — fused to its brain (any of eleven LLM providers, cloud or fully local) and its senses (your data sources, spoken natively over MCP).

The senses are the point. A tool is something you pick up; a sense is something you are born with. The reasoning engine is co-designed with the data source — which is why a Uderia agent understands your schema before it writes a single query.

And it remembers — local per-agent memory, global shared memory, situational awareness per session.

Act IIAt Home

It lives where your data lives.

Your data stays where you put it; the plan is what travels. This agent stays home — and still reasons like the cloud.

Outside the trust story: this act is about where the agent runs.

The agent's habitat is your estate: your servers, your cloud tenancy, or a laptop with no network at all. Uderia decouples strategic intelligence from execution — proven plans, learned from champion cases, let a local model perform like a hyperscaler while raw data never crosses the perimeter.

Run it air-gapped with local models and local stores — zero external calls. Or let a cloud brain reason over plans while execution stays sovereign. Either way: your keys, your rules, your ground.

Act IVIn Check

Watch it refuse.

Watch an agent being stopped — and verify that it was, cryptographically.

In the trust story: Act II — observe → enforce → prove and Act III — the loop closes.

Every turn runs a gauntlet: injections refused at the door, autonomous authority checked before every tool, the answer screened for personal data — and a grounding gate that withholds a confident answer built on data outside the agent's declared scope.

Then the turn is sealed: hash-chained, Ed25519-signed, verifiable offline by anyone. One key signs three facts — what it did, how good it was, what it is made of. And its measured conduct feeds back into its trust badge: an agent that misbehaves is visibly, automatically less trusted.

Signed · enforced · proved · kept in check — the full story lives at trust.uderia.com
